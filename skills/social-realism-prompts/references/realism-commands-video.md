# Realism Commands — Video (30 snippets, 7 categories)

Modular snippets for video prompts. Choose only cues justified by the scene, device, and
provider. There is no minimum category count. Keep generation footage stable and reserve
deliberate degradation for post-processing notes.

Categories: Face & skin · Body · Camera movement · Lighting · Device · Editing · Other

---

## Face & skin

- **Micro facial movements**: `subtle facial motion, micro-expressions, natural blinking`
- **Asymmetric blinking**: `natural asymmetric blinking, subtle facial tics, unscripted micro-movements`

## Body

- **Natural body movement**: `natural body movement, casual gestures, unplanned motion`
- **Interrupted action**: `interrupted action, stop-and-go motion, casual pacing`
- **Weight shifting**: `weight shifting between legs while standing and talking, casual natural sway`
- **Clothing adjustment**: `mid-sentence clothing adjustment, unplanned casual gesture`

## Camera movement

- **Shaky camera**: `handheld camera shake, slight jitter, imperfect stabilization`
- **Abrupt movements**: `sudden camera movements, quick movements`
- **Real-time reframing**: `camera reframing mid-shot, small corrections, unplanned adjustments`
- **Accidental zoom**: `slight accidental zoom in or out, unsteady handheld adjustment`
- **Frame cutoff while walking**: `frame cutting off parts of the body while walking, imperfect handheld tracking`
- **Surface vibration**: `surface vibration transmitted to camera, slight rattling shake from table or car ride`

## Lighting

- **Light variation**: `lighting changes mid-shot, exposure shifts, inconsistent brightness`
- **Unstable exposure**: `auto exposure adjustment, brightness pulsing, some natural exposure fluctuations`
- **Passing shadow**: `passing shadow crossing the scene, brief natural light interruption`
- **Flickering background light**: `flickering background light, inconsistent glow from screen or sign`

## Device

- **Phone video**: `smartphone video quality, compressed footage, digital artifacts`
- **Video noise**: `video noise, grain in motion, low-light artifacts`
- **Phone cadence**: `typical phone-video cadence at the provider-supported frame rate, natural motion blur, no cinematic slow motion`
- **Rear camera low light**: `rear camera struggling in low light, grainy image, focus hunting`
- **Vertical story format**: `vertical smartphone story format, slightly cropped framing`
- **Autofocus hunting on face**: `autofocus hunting and refocusing on the face repeatedly, brief soft blur pulses`

## Editing

- **Abrupt start and end**: `abrupt start and end, no fade in or out`
- **Irregular pacing**: `uneven pacing, natural pauses, no jump cuts`
- **Authentic amateur video**: `authentic amateur video, everyday realism, not cinematic`
- **Abrupt edit**: `post-processing note: end on a natural sentence fragment with a hard cut, no fade`
- **Social media compression**: `post-processing note: mild re-upload compression and slight blockiness while keeping faces and audio intelligible`

## Other

- **No production aesthetic**: `no cinematic lighting, no professional filming, no stylized look`
- **Filmer's reflection**: `accidental reflection of the person filming visible in a window or mirror`
