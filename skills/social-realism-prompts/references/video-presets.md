# Video Presets (6)

Adaptable text-to-video prompts. For an authorized video-to-video transformation, begin
with `Preserve the people, action, timing, and factual content; change only the capture
treatment as follows:`.

Video realism hinges on motion: continuous non-looping movement, unscripted rhythm,
camera movement with a physical cause, and plausible exposure behavior. Select FPS from
the intended phone/platform and provider profile rather than forcing 24 FPS.

## Contents

1. Home vlog
2. Street vlog (walking selfie)
3. Casual bar/restaurant capture
4. Driving vlog
5. Gym workout video
6. Cooking video

---

## 1. Home vlog

On the couch, telling a story with excited energy, continuous fluid movement, warm room
light, typical phone-video cadence, and natural speech rhythm.

```
Amateur home vlog video, adult sitting on a couch talking directly to camera, telling a story with excited energy. Continuous fluid movements flow into each other without an obvious gesture loop. Leans forward to emphasize a point then settles back, runs fingers through hair while thinking, looks briefly to the side while remembering something. Natural hand and hair motion blur. Warm living-room ambient light. Typical 30fps phone-video cadence unless the provider profile requires another rate. Natural speech rhythm with pauses, like a video call with a close friend. Minimally edited footage, no cinematic grade.
```

## 2. Street vlog (walking selfie)

Selfie walking down the sidewalk, camera shakes with each step, glances away at pedestrians and back, auto-exposure shifting between sun and shadow.

```
Street vlog selfie, adult walking on a busy sidewalk holding the phone in selfie mode. Camera movement follows the person's walking rhythm. Gestures while talking, glances away to navigate around pedestrians, then back to camera. Brings the phone slightly closer for emphasis. Moving background - people, cars, buildings. Auto-exposure shifts between sun and building shadows. Plausible motion blur, no obvious repeated gesture loop. Typical 30fps phone-video cadence unless the provider profile requires another rate. Breathless, minimally produced walking-vlog energy.
```

## 3. Casual bar/restaurant capture

Handheld video at a restaurant, person never looks at the camera, animated off-screen conversation, dramatic expressions, light/focus shifting during the scene.

```
Candid handheld video of an adult at a restaurant, engaged in an animated conversation with consenting companions off-screen. They mostly look toward the conversation rather than the camera. Expression changes naturally; hand gestures punctuate the story without an obvious loop. The filmer makes small angle corrections with subtle handheld drift. Typical 30fps phone-video cadence with motion blur appropriate to quick gestures. Exposure and focus adjust slightly during the scene. Minimally edited dinner-party footage; natural camera movement, not cinematic coverage.
```

## 4. Driving vlog

Secure fixed dashboard mount, attentive driving with both hands on the wheel, shifting
windshield glare, and road vibration.

```
Amateur driving vlog video recorded from a securely fixed dashboard mount. Adult driver keeps both hands on the wheel and attention on the road, speaking casually without touching or adjusting the camera. Sunlight glare shifts across the windshield and face as the car turns, with natural exposure fluctuation. Road and passing scenery blur through the side window. Casual speech rhythm with pauses. Slight camera vibration from road bumps. Typical 30fps phone-video cadence, minimally edited dashcam-adjacent quality, no cinematic grade.
```

## 5. Gym workout video

Phone propped against equipment, mid-set breathing heavily, harsh gym lighting, blurred gymgoers in background.

```
Amateur gym workout video, phone secured on a small stand near the equipment, adult mid-set or between reps, breathing heavily. Continuous natural movement - adjusting grip, wiping sweat, glancing at the camera between sets. Overhead gym fluorescent lighting, consenting or unidentifiable gymgoers softened in the background. Slight motion blur during movement; a small vibration if the stand is bumped. Natural pauses to catch breath. Typical 30fps phone-video cadence, minimally edited smartphone footage, no cinematic polish.
```

## 6. Cooking video

Phone on a small tripod on the counter, talking while chopping/stirring, steam softening the image, cluttered counter.

```
Amateur cooking video, phone secured on a small tripod on the kitchen counter, adult talking to the camera while chopping, stirring, or plating food. Frequent glances down at the task then back up, with natural pauses. Steam and warm kitchen light slightly soften the image; exposure adjusts near the stove. Hands enter and leave frame, with ingredients and utensils on the counter. Slight motion blur on hand movements. Typical 30fps phone-video cadence, minimally edited home-kitchen footage, no cinematic grade.
```
