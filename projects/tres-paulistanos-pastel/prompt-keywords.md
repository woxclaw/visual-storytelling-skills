# Prompt keyword library

## Global style phrase

Contemporary rear-smartphone observational footage, natural digital detail, moderate
phone-camera depth, preserved skin texture, slightly warm neutral daylight, moderate
saturation, open shadows, gentle highlight rolloff, minimally produced and unstaged.

## Location vocabulary

- **Bixiga / Praça Dom Orione:** Sunday neighborhood antiques fair, uneven stone paving,
  compact vendor canopies, low-rise Bela Vista fabric, warm late-morning daylight,
  ordinary foot traffic, no invented readable signs.
- **Praça da Sé:** broad civic square, Catedral da Sé recognizable at a respectful
  distance, grey paving, diffuse late-morning light, ordinary central-city movement,
  a small fictional licensed street-food setup, no claim of a named weekly fair.
- **MASP / Avenida Paulista:** red MASP structural elements and open public span,
  Paulista pedestrian movement, antiques-fair tables as soft background context,
  bright overcast-to-sun daylight, no invented museum branding or readable signage.

## Lighting conditions

- **Late morning:** soft-bright São Paulo daylight, small exposure corrections as the
  operator reframes, no dramatic golden-hour treatment.
- **Open shade:** face in soft open shade, brighter square or avenue behind, automatic
  smartphone exposure keeps skin readable without erasing the background.

## Capture vocabulary

Handheld by a nearby consenting interviewer, small breathing drift, one restrained
reframe, natural blinking, weight shift, ordinary gesture, 30 fps phone-video cadence,
speech remains the dominant action, no repeated gesture loop.

## Global negative constraints

- no brands, logos, watermarks, labels or generated text
- no identifiable incidental bystanders
- no glamour retouching, plastic skin or shallow DSLR bokeh
- no advertising food styling or immaculate studio light
- no invented news graphics, microphones or documentary network marks
- no European Portuguese speech and no English speech
- no fake claim that a specific vendor or recurring food fair exists at the location
