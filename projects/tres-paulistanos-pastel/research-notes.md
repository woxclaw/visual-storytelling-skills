# Location research notes

- São Paulo's Subprefeitura Sé lists long-running Sunday arts/antiques fairs at Praça
  Dom Orione in Bixiga and at MASP's open span. This supports the background context,
  but not the existence of a specific pastel stall:
  https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/se/w/noticias/9621
- The city describes Praça Dom Orione as a Bixiga gathering place that receives the
  traditional antiques fair on weekends:
  https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/se/w/noticias/118041
- MASP describes its open span as a public square. The film uses the architecture as
  recognizable context without implying museum endorsement:
  https://assets.masp.org.br/pdf/FOLDER_MASP-Bem-vindo_PT.pdf
- The city maintains separate legal treatment for street-food activity. The Praça da
  Sé setup is therefore explicitly fictional and generic, not a claim about a weekly
  registered food fair at that exact point:
  https://legislacao.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/lei-15947-de-26-de-dezembro-de-2013
